## 6. Conclusion

The USB drive image associated with both cases task 1 and 2 were examined and they were found to contain relative files, as a result of carrying out keyword search and review of the content, the files were found to match the description. According to the evidences, the USB drives have files which match the confidential documents. What was found support the worry that the former employees have sensitive company data on that drive.
